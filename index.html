Discover the Dominican Republic

Where Caribbean beaches, vibrant culture, rich history, and unforgettable adventures come together.

Top three activities in DR

Caribbean Paradise

Punta Cana is one of the Dominican Republic's best-known destinations, famous for its white-sand beaches, turquoise waters, resorts, excursions, and nightlife. The tourism board describes more than 50 km of beaches in the region.

Where History Comes Alive

Santo Domingo combines modern city life with the historic Zona Colonial, where visitors can explore landmarks such as the Catedral Primada de América, Alcázar de Colón and Calle Las Damas.

Nature at Its Best.

Samaná is a great section for showing the country's natural side. Think tropical beaches, lush mountains, waterfalls, boat excursions and coastal landscapes.
